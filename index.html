School Heritage & Philosophy

Giving Back to the Land: From a Featured School to an Educational Brand

Founded on August 1, 2009 by the Chiayi County Government in gratitude for Formosa Plastics Group founder Wang Yung-ching's dedication to improving local education, Yung-Ching High School provides an uninterrupted 6-year junior and senior high curriculum.

Embracing future competencies, our mission focuses on "Global Vision, Local Care, Forward-Looking Innovation, and Holistic Education". Entering the 115th academic year, we have established three key structural balances:

Upward & Inward

Academics reach upward while internal self-worth and resilience remain grounded.

Forward & Deep

Cutting-edge technology accelerates progress, while the humanities inspire deep empathy.

Outward & Resilient

Global exchange expands horizons, and athletics build grit against adversity.
